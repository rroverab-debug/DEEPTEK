Many small businesses in Doha try a little of everything: a few social posts, a boosted post, maybe a Google ad. Without a plan, it is hard to tell what is working. Here is a simple order of steps that suits most small businesses in Qatar.

## Step 1: Get the foundations right

Before spending on ads, make sure people can find and contact you.

- **Google Business Profile.** Create and verify it, add accurate hours, photos and your area, and keep it updated.
- **A clear website.** It should explain what you offer in a few seconds, work well on phones and make it easy to call, WhatsApp or book.
- **Consistent details.** Your business name, number and location should match on Google, social media and your website.

## Step 2: Choose one or two social channels

You do not need to be on every platform. Pick the one or two where your customers already are, and post consistently.

- Restaurants, cafes, salons and shops often do well on Instagram, with real photos and short videos.
- Business services usually do better on LinkedIn, with useful advice and case studies.
- Some audiences are very active on Snapchat and TikTok, which suit short, informal video.

Plan your posts a month ahead, and include dates that matter locally, such as Ramadan, Eid and Qatar National Day.

## Step 3: Speak to both languages

Qatar has a large Arabic-speaking population and a large expatriate community. Write separate Arabic and English versions of your key messages and ads rather than translating word for word. The offer can stay the same, but the wording should feel natural to each audience.

## Step 4: Start small with paid ads

When the foundations are ready, test a small budget:

- **Google Search ads** for people already searching for what you offer, such as "cafe in West Bay" or "IT support Doha".
- **Social ads** to reach new people in a specific area or age group.

Run a few ads at a time, give them enough time to gather results, then keep what works and stop what does not.

## Step 5: Make WhatsApp part of the plan

Many customers in Qatar prefer to message rather than call. Add a WhatsApp button to your website and ads, reply quickly, and consider automating common questions such as opening hours, menus or prices, while keeping a person available for anything important.

## Step 6: Measure enquiries, not just likes

Likes and followers are encouraging, but enquiries pay the bills. Each month, check:

- How many calls, WhatsApp messages, form submissions and bookings you received.
- Which channel each one came from.
- How much you spent per enquiry on each paid channel.

Then move your time and budget towards what brings real customers.
