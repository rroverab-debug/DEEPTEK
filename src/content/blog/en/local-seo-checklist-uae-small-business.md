When someone in Dubai searches "cafe near me" or "IT support in Sharjah", Google shows a map with a few businesses and then a list of websites. Local SEO is the work that helps your business appear in those results.

You do not need a big budget to get the basics right. Use this checklist to see where you stand.

## 1. Google Business Profile

This is the single most important step for local searches.

- Create or claim your profile and complete the verification.
- Choose the most accurate main category, then add secondary ones.
- Add your opening hours, including public holidays and Ramadan hours.
- Add real photos of your premises, team and products. Update them regularly.
- Write a clear description of what you do and the areas you serve.
- Use the posts feature to share offers, events and news.

## 2. Consistent name, address and phone number

Your business name, address and phone number should be written the same way everywhere: your website, Google, Apple Maps, Bing, social media and directories. Small differences confuse search engines and customers.

## 3. Reviews

Reviews influence both rankings and the decision to visit.

- Ask happy customers for a review, at the right moment, with a direct link.
- Reply to every review, positive or negative, politely and quickly.
- Never buy reviews or post fake ones. It breaks Google's rules and can get your profile suspended.

## 4. A website that matches what people search

- Give each main service its own page, with a clear title such as "Website design in Abu Dhabi".
- Write page titles and descriptions for every page. They are what people see in Google.
- Mention the areas you serve naturally in your text, such as Dubai Marina or Al Ain, where it is true.
- Add structured data so search engines understand your business type, location and contact details.
- Make sure your phone number and WhatsApp link work with one tap on mobile.

## 5. English and Arabic

Many UAE customers search in Arabic. A bilingual website with separate English and Arabic pages, linked correctly so Google knows they are translations, lets you appear in both sets of results. Your Google Business Profile description and posts can also be written in both languages.

## 6. Speed and mobile experience

Most local searches happen on phones, often on the move. A page that takes several seconds to load loses visitors before they see your offer. Compress images, avoid heavy plugins and test your site on a real phone using mobile data.

## 7. Helpful content

Articles that answer real customer questions, such as "how much does catering cost for 50 people?" or "what should a small office IT setup include?", bring in visitors who are already looking for what you offer. One useful article each month is better than many thin ones.

## 8. Measure what matters

Connect Google Search Console to see which searches show your site, and use analytics to see which pages lead to calls, WhatsApp messages and form enquiries. Rankings are useful, but enquiries are what pay the bills.
